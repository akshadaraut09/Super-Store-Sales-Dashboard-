# Super-Store-Sales-Dashboard-
Interactive Sales Dashboard built using Power BI Public select 
An interactive Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard built to analyze Super Store sales performance and identify important business trends.

 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data to understand sales, profit, quantity, customer segments, product categories, regions, and payment methods.
The dashboard converts raw sales data into interactive visualizations and KPIs, making it easier to understand business performance and identify areas of improvement.
For your Super Store Sales Power BI project, you can mention these KPIs in your README:

 📊Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Sales – Overall revenue generated from all orders.
Total Profit – Total profit generated from sales.
Total Quantity – Total number of products sold.
Average Delivery Time – Average number of days taken to deliver orders.
Sales by Region – Sales performance across different regions.
Sales by Category – Revenue generated by each product category.
Sales by Customer Segment – Sales contribution from Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
Sales by Payment Mode – Distribution of sales across different payment methods.
Sales Trend –Monthly/yearly changes in sales performance.
Sales Forecast – Expected future sales based on historical sales trends.
